# Design direction — Goods

## World

A quiet independent goods journal: warm paper, dark espresso lettering, soft stone and taupe product stages, and a restrained rust accent.

## Type and layout

Use an expressive editorial serif for the lead and a composed sans-serif for controls and product facts. Let the product stage take real space; keep the shopping path obvious and the catalog easy to scan.

## Interaction

Search, filters, product details, bag edits, sign-in, and demo checkout remain direct and keyboard accessible. Theme changes preserve readable contrast.
