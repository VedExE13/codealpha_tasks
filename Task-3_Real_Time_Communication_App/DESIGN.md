# Design direction — Open Room

## World

A quiet night-mode communication suite: ink-black foundations, indigo room surfaces, restrained electric-blue controls, and cool lavender status text.

## Type and layout

Use a confident geometric sans for room and panel titles and a compact sans for status and call controls. Keep the active room, people, and current activity easy to locate; let chat and the whiteboard own their work area.

## Interaction

Joining, copy code, invite link, people, leave, and delete remain distinct. Microphone and camera are off until the user enables them. Whiteboard controls stay visible and keyboard reachable.

## Reference translation

The banking-dashboard reference contributes its dark indigo and cobalt information hierarchy. Financial values, transactions, and assistant claims are not imported into the communication product.
